Tier 1 Access

The Opportunity

SHER is a conservation-led residential and experiential development on a pristine coastal estate in Saint Lucia. The project combines a luxury boutique lodge, 20 for-sale residences, and a permanently protected wetland sanctuary — all designed to create irreplaceable scarcity value in a market that rewards authenticity.

204,148 sf Total estate — 4.69 acres
108,774 sf Developable land — 2.50 acres
95,374 sf Conservation zone — 2.19 acres (46.7% of estate)
20 Residences across three categories
US$3,500,000 Working capital target
US$10,200,000 Base for-sale revenue
2.9× Gross revenue-to-capital ratio (approx.)
12–18 mo Construction phase per phase

The estate occupies a rare coastal position — bounded on one side by the Caribbean Sea, on another by a protected mangrove estuary, and on a third by hills that create a natural enclosure for the sanctuary. The combination of buildable upland and permanently non-buildable wetland creates a moat around the residential parcels that no future competitor can replicate. That is the foundation of the value proposition.

SHER does not compete on category with conventional resort development. Every material, every form, every experience is designed to deepen the connection between guests, residents and the living ecosystem they are entering. This is not a selling line — it is a structural commitment enforced by legal land covenant and reflected in the building specification from the ground up.

Construction approach: Hemp block, compressed earth, clay brick, lime render, reclaimed timber, and locally sourced stone are the primary building materials. This is not a novelty — these materials have a thousand-year structural track record, an ultra-low embodied carbon footprint, and an exceptional thermal performance in tropical climates. Independent structural certifications are available at Tier 2.

Capital Structure

~2.9× Gross revenue-to-capital ratio at base model

Investor note: The US$3.5M working capital figure is the reference for equity and/or senior debt participation. This funds the construction phase of the 20 residences and supporting infrastructure on the estate. Hospitality operations (lodge, restaurant) are capitalised and operated separately and are not included in these figures.
