How We Operate

Environmental Policy

We operate inside a RAMSAR-designated wetland. That is not a marketing advantage — it is a responsibility. These are the standards we hold ourselves to on every visit.

01
Leave No Trace
Nothing enters the bay that doesn't leave with us. All waste — including food packaging, equipment wrapping, and any incidental litter — is returned to shore. Guides conduct a final check of all guest areas before departure. Zero waste is not an aspiration at SHER — it is a condition of every tour.
02
Reef-Safe Products Only
All sunscreen, insect repellent, and personal care products used during SHER experiences must be reef-safe and ocean-safe. Guests are informed of this requirement at booking. SHER supplies reef-safe sunscreen on request. Products containing oxybenzone, octinoxate, and other reef-damaging chemicals are not permitted on the water.
03
Mangrove Buffer Zones
SHER guides observe strict buffer distances from mangrove root systems. Kayaks and canoes are not permitted to enter the root zone or make physical contact with mangrove vegetation. The mangrove corridor is a marine nursery for seventeen species of fish. We observe it — we do not disturb it. Anchoring is never done within the root system.
04
Scorpion Islet Landing Limits
SHER permits a maximum of one private landing per day on Scorpion Islet, with a group size limit of four guests per landing. Landings are made at the designated beach access point only. No guest may enter the interior vegetation, disturb nesting areas, or remove any natural material from the island. The PSEPA Management Plan designates guided access from Savannes Bay as an approved activity — SHER complies with all conditions of that approval.
05
Zero Single-Use Plastics
No single-use plastic is permitted on any SHER experience. Water is provided in reusable or compostable vessels. Champagne and beverages are served without plastic straws. Food and refreshments are presented in biodegradable or reusable packaging. This is a strict operational rule — not a preference — and applies to all guides, staff, and guests.
06
Sea Moss Community Support
Sea moss cultivation in Savannes Bay provides livelihoods for families in the Micoud community. SHER conducts respectful, non-contact observation of active sea moss cultivation zones. Kayaks and canoes maintain safe distance from cultivation lines and equipment. SHER actively supports sea moss farming families through local employment and by incorporating sea moss products in our guest experiences wherever possible.

We are guests of this ecosystem — not its owners.

Savannes Bay was protected long before SHER existed, and it will be protected long after. Our role is to provide considered access — to share what makes this place extraordinary without diminishing what makes it so. Every decision we make on the water is made with that responsibility in mind.
