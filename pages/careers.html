Work on the bay

We are building something rare — a conservation-led ecotourism operation on one of the Caribbean's most protected bays. If that matters to you, we would like to meet you.

Who We Are Looking For

People who care about this place

SHER is a small, intentional team. We are not looking for tourism industry veterans who will bring resort habits to the bay. We are looking for people who understand that Savannes Bay is extraordinary — and who want to be part of protecting it while sharing it.

We train every guide ourselves. We set our own standards, higher than the industry minimum. We work early mornings and we work in the heat. And we believe completely in what we are doing.

Open Roles

Current opportunities

Eco Guide
Full-time · Micoud, Saint Lucia

Lead guided kayak and canoe tours on Savannes Bay. Interpret the bay's ecology, enforce the Conservation Code, and deliver an experience that guests remember for years. You are the reason guests return.

We are looking for
  • Strong swimmer; comfortable on open water in all conditions
  • Genuine knowledge of or interest in the bay's ecology
  • Calm, authoritative, and warm — in that order
  • Saint Lucian or resident with right to work
  • First aid certification preferred — we will support you to obtain it
Guest Experience & Concierge
Part-time · Remote & On-site

Handle enquiries, coordinate bookings, and ensure every guest arrives prepared and every resort partner is looked after. You are the first person guests hear from and the reason their expectations are managed well before they reach the bay.

We are looking for
  • Excellent written English — clear, warm, and professional
  • Organised and reliable with email and WhatsApp communication
  • Experience in hospitality, travel, or customer-facing roles preferred
  • Availability during early mornings for pre-tour coordination
Bay Operations & Maintenance
Part-time · Micoud, Saint Lucia

Keep the bay base running — equipment checks, kayak and canoe maintenance, safety gear inspection, and pre-tour setup. You are the person nobody sees, but everything depends on you. The guide can only deliver because you have done your work first.

We are looking for
  • Mechanically minded — experience with small watercraft preferred
  • Early riser; comfortable starting before 4:30 AM on tour days
  • Based in or near Micoud
  • Dependable — this role is the foundation everything else sits on
