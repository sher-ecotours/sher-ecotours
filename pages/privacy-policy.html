04

Payment Processing — FYGARO

Online payments for SHER experiences are processed by FYGARO, a certified Caribbean payment gateway. When you click "Proceed to Payment," you are redirected to FYGARO's secure hosted checkout environment. All card data is collected, encrypted, and processed by FYGARO under their own Privacy Policy and PCI DSS compliance obligations.

SHER does not receive, transmit, or store cardholder data. The only payment information we hold is the booking confirmation reference FYGARO returns to us on successful payment.

FYGARO is the data processor for payment transactions. By proceeding to payment, you also agree to FYGARO's terms and privacy policy as shown on their checkout page.

05

Who We Share Your Data With

We share your personal data only where necessary:

  • FYGARO — payment processing (name, booking amount, email for receipt)
  • Supabase — our secure booking database, hosted in the European Union, used to store and manage booking records
  • Your SHER guide — name, number of guests, occasion details, and any special requests necessary to prepare your experience
  • Regulatory authorities — if required by law (e.g., tax authority, Saint Lucia Tourism Authority)

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

07

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure — ask us to delete your data (subject to legal retention obligations)
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we use your data
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Withdrawal of consent — withdraw any consent you have given at any time

To exercise any right, email us at bookings@shersanctuary.com with the subject line "Data Request". We will respond within 30 days.

09

Security

This website is served exclusively over HTTPS (TLS 1.2+), encrypting all data in transit between your browser and our servers. Booking data is stored in Supabase, which enforces row-level security, encrypted storage at rest, and is SOC 2 Type II certified.

Payment data never touches SHER's servers. FYGARO is responsible for PCI DSS compliance for all card transactions.

While we take reasonable precautions, no internet transmission is 100% secure. If you believe your data has been compromised, contact us immediately at bookings@shersanctuary.com.
