About SHER

For media use

SHER · Eco Sanctuary is a guided eco-experience operator based on the shores of Savannes Bay, Micoud, Saint Lucia. Founded on the conviction that considered access to one of the Caribbean's most protected coastal ecosystems can be transformative for guests and community alike, SHER currently operates three signature experiences on the bay.

Savannes Bay sits within the Pointe Sable Environmental Protection Area (PSEPA), gazetted in 2007, and holds RAMSAR Wetland designation — one of only two such sites in all of Saint Lucia. SHER is the only formally approved guided operator for kayak and canoe tours from Savannes Bay to Scorpion Islet.

SHER is operated by Safeport Security Services Limited, Saint Lucia.
