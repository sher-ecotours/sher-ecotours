How payment works

1

Choose & Fill Your Details

Select your experience, preferred date, number of guests, and any special requests.

2

Proceed to FYGARO Checkout

Click "Proceed to Payment" to be redirected to FYGARO's secure hosted checkout page. Your booking summary is pre-filled.

3

Pay Securely

Enter your card details on FYGARO's encrypted page. SHER never sees your card number. Major cards accepted.

4

Confirmation Within 4 Hours

You'll receive an immediate payment receipt from FYGARO, and a personal confirmation from your SHER guide within 4 hours.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

Cancellations made 72 hours or more before your experience receive a full refund. Cancellations within 72 hours are non-refundable, though we will work with you to reschedule. In the event of weather or safety cancellation by SHER, you will receive a full refund or a complimentary reschedule.
