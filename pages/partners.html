Partner Tiers

Commission structure

Your commission tier is calculated on confirmed bookings completed in the prior calendar month. Tiers reset on the 1st of each month.

Partner Tier Bookings Per Month Commission Rate
Standard Partner 1–5 bookings 12%
Preferred Partner 6–12 bookings 15%
Elite Partner 13+ bookings 18%
Commission is paid on the 1st of every month for all bookings completed in the prior month. No chasing required — we pay automatically.
