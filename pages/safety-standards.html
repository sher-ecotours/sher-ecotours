Our Commitment

Safety Standards

Every SHER experience operates under formally certified safety protocols. Your safety is the non-negotiable condition of everything we do on the water.

Operated under required permits and safety standards. SHER operates under required permits, Saint Lucia Tourism Authority registration, and Coast Guard safety compliance. All guides hold current water safety certification. Documentation available on request.
Coast Guard Compliant Equipment
All watercraft and safety equipment meets Saint Lucia Coast Guard requirements.
  • Certified personal flotation devices (PFDs) for every guest
  • Sit-on-top kayaks and traditional wooden canoes — stable, low-capsizing risk
  • Whistle and signalling equipment on all vessels
  • First aid kit carried on every tour
  • Equipment inspected before each departure
Certified Guides
Every SHER guide is locally trained and certified.
  • Water safety and first aid certification — current and renewed annually
  • Deep knowledge of Savannes Bay, tides, and seasonal conditions
  • Briefed on each guest group before departure
  • Trained in environmental protocols and protected area conduct
Pre-Departure Briefing
Every guest receives a full safety briefing before entering the water.
  • Equipment fitting and adjustment for each guest individually
  • Paddling technique and canoe balance instruction
  • Route overview, stop points, and duration
  • Conduct guidelines for the protected area
  • Emergency signal and communication procedures
Radio Communication
SHER maintains continuous radio contact throughout all water activities.
  • VHF marine radio carried on all tours
  • Shore contact maintained for the duration of each experience
  • Coast Guard channel monitored at all times on the water
  • Emergency call procedures rehearsed by all guides
Weather Policy
No tour proceeds in unsafe conditions. Safety takes precedence over schedule.
  • Wind and sea state assessed each morning before confirmation
  • Tours cancelled or rescheduled if conditions are below standard
  • Full refund or rescheduling offered for weather cancellations
  • Lightning and storm protocols — immediate return to shore
Emergency Procedures
Guides are trained and rehearsed for on-water emergencies.
  • Man-overboard and capsize recovery protocols
  • Medical emergency response — first aid on water and shore
  • Emergency contact numbers carried on all tours
  • Nearest medical facility: Victoria Hospital, Castries (approx. 35 min)
  • Coast Guard coordination — direct channel and GPS positioning
