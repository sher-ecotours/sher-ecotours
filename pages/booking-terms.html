Everything you need to know before you arrive. These terms apply to all SHER Sanctuary Experiences.

01

Booking & Payment

All SHER experiences are arranged by private appointment. There are no walk-in tours. Once a booking enquiry is received, we confirm availability and send a secure payment link within 24 hours.

Deposit: A 50% deposit is required to hold your date. The remaining balance is due 48 hours before your experience. For bookings made within 48 hours of the experience date, full payment is required at the time of booking.

Payment method: Payment is processed securely through FYGARO. We accept all major credit and debit cards. No cash or bank transfers are accepted for advance bookings.

Group minimum: Bay Serenity · Golden Mirror and Bay Serenity · Calm Reflections require a minimum of 2 guests to operate. Scorpio's Secret and Scorpio's Sanctuary are private by design — no minimum guest count applies.

02

Cancellation Policy

We understand that travel plans change. The following policy applies to all cancellations initiated by the guest.

Notice given Refund
7 days or more before Full refund of all amounts paid
48 hours to 7 days before Full refund of deposit; balance not charged
Less than 48 hours before Deposit is non-refundable; balance not charged
No-show (no prior notice) Full booking amount is forfeited

To cancel, contact us by email at bookings@shersanctuary.com or via WhatsApp. Cancellations are only confirmed once acknowledged by SHER in writing.

Rescheduling: We will always do our best to accommodate a date change at no additional charge, subject to availability. Contact us as early as possible and we will find an alternative that works.

03

Weather & Conditions Policy

Savannes Bay operates in a protected coastal environment. The bay is calm year-round, and tours proceed in most weather conditions. However, guest safety is our first responsibility.

SHER reserves the right to cancel or postpone any tour if, in the sole judgement of the guide, conditions on the water present a safety risk. This includes but is not limited to: sustained winds above 25 knots, lightning within the tour area, or sea conditions that make paddling unsafe for the group.

If SHER cancels due to weather: You will receive a full refund of all amounts paid, or the option to reschedule at no cost. We will notify you as early as possible — typically by 4:00 AM for sunrise experiences and 2 hours before departure for afternoon experiences.

If you choose to cancel due to weather forecast: Standard cancellation terms apply. We encourage guests to wait for our assessment, as conditions on the bay are often calmer than surrounding areas suggest.

Sunrise experiences: The Golden Mirror tour departs at 5:15 AM. Weather assessment is made the evening prior and again at 4:00 AM. Guests will always be notified before they need to leave their accommodation.

04

Health & Fitness Requirements

Our kayak and canoe experiences are designed to be accessible to most adults in reasonable health. You do not need to be an experienced paddler — your guide will brief you fully before departure.

You should not participate if you: have a heart condition, severe back or joint problems, or are in the third trimester of pregnancy, without first consulting your physician and informing SHER at the time of booking.

All guests must: be able to swim or be comfortable on the water wearing a life vest, follow guide instructions at all times, and be physically capable of paddling for the duration of the experience.

Children under 12 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian and must wear a life vest at all times on the water. Please inform us of the ages of any children in your group when booking.

05

Conservation Conditions

By confirming a booking with SHER, every guest accepts and agrees to observe the SHER Conservation Code in full. These are not guidelines — they are conditions of participation.

The Conservation Code prohibits, among other things: unsanctioned landings on Scorpion Islet, contact with mangrove root systems, disturbance of wildlife, and introduction of waste into the bay environment. Violations may result in immediate termination of the experience without refund.

Scorpion Islet sits within the Pointe Sable Environmental Protection Area (PSEPA). SHER holds a formal access agreement for this site. Unsanctioned landings by any person — including tour guests acting independently — may constitute a breach of PSEPA regulations.

06

Liability & Risk

SHER Sanctuary Experiences operates in full compliance with Saint Lucia Coast Guard safety requirements. All equipment is inspected before each tour. All guides are trained in water safety and first aid.

Participation in any SHER experience involves inherent risks associated with outdoor water activities. By booking, guests acknowledge these risks and agree that SHER, its guides, and its operators shall not be liable for any injury, loss, or damage sustained during the experience, except where caused by the negligence of SHER staff.
