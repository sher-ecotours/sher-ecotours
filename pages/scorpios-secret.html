A traditional wooden canoe. A guide who knows when to speak and when to disappear into the rhythm of the paddle. The mangrove corridor at its most silent. And at the end of it — a small, sandy beach on a protected island that most people in Saint Lucia have never set foot on.

Scorpio's Secret is not a tour. It is a setting — arranged around you, timed around you, and held completely private for you. Whatever the occasion, whatever the words you have been saving, they will find their moment here.

We do not pair strangers. We do not run a schedule. We arrange one experience per day, and we give it everything.

The Destination

Scorpion Islet —
your private beach

A protected nature reserve sitting in the heart of the Pointe Sable Environmental Protection Area. Sandy, quiet, fringed by mangroves, and accessible only by water. Under normal circumstances, it is rarely visited. For Scorpio's Secret, we arrange one private landing per day — yours.

How We Operate

Three commitments
we never break

01
"Strangers are never paired together."
Every booking is entirely private. We do not fill spaces, we do not add guests, and we do not combine reservations. When you book, the experience is yours alone.
02
"One landing per day. Maximum."
Scorpion Islet receives one private visit per day through SHER. Not because the island limits us — because the experience demands it. A private beach is only private when it is.
03
"The guide steps back when the moment calls for it."
Our guides are trained in discretion. They know when presence means help and when it means intrusion. On this experience, they disappear when you need them to.
