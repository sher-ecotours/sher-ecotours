The Concept

A table on the
open water

Table d'Eau is SHER's private dining collection — two distinct experiences at the water's edge of Savannes Bay, each designed around a specific moment of the day.

L'Aube is a private sunrise breakfast. Guests arrive before dawn, watch the sun rise over the bay from an upper deck, then descend to a hardwood table set directly on the volcanic rock at the water's edge — at the moment the gold light softens into morning.

L'Estuaire is a private bay table set inside the south mangrove arch — enclosed on three sides by living mangrove canopy, open to the bay on the fourth. Available at midday and late afternoon. All sittings close by 5:30pm as the bay returns to its wildlife.

One table. Two guests. One sitting at a time. Never shared.

Table d'Eau opens in 2026. Join the Early Access list to be contacted personally before public booking opens.
