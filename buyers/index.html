The Cottage Collection

La Collection Papiyòt

Ten cottages. Ten butterflies. One sanctuary.

Papiyòt is the Saint Lucian Kwéyòl word for butterfly — distinct from papiyon, which denotes a moth. Each of the ten cottages carries the name of a butterfly recorded in Saint Lucia, giving every residence a memorable identity, a colour story and a conservation narrative. The butterfly identity is permanently fixed to its cottage — it is not a buyer selection.

Savannes Bay

A RAMSAR-designated wetland of international importance. One of Saint Lucia’s most ecologically significant and least-discovered coastal sites — and the land on which SHER Sanctuary is built.

Closer Than You Think

Savannes Bay sits at the southern tip of Saint Lucia, 15 minutes by road from Hewanorra International Airport. For a guest arriving from Miami, London, Toronto, or New York, the journey from plane to bay is one of the shortest in the Caribbean — no two-hour transfer, no winding mountain road. Direct access to a protected wetland estate on arrival.

The Right Moment to Own Here

Savannes Bay is entering a period of considered, high-quality development. A major five-star resort is currently under construction on the shared oceanfront a short distance away — bringing international visitor traffic and hospitality infrastructure to a coastline that has historically been known only to those who looked for it.

SHER residences placed in the HOA rental pool are managed, maintained, and marketed to guests who have already chosen this part of Saint Lucia. Owners who prefer personal use retain priority access. The structure is designed to serve both.

This is not a speculative position. It is an early, well-located stake in an area that institutional hospitality investment has already validated.

Living at SHER Sanctuary

01

The Arrival

The gate at SHER is made of stone and timber — restrained, unhurried, nothing announcing arrival except the change in atmosphere. The path narrows past a screen of palms, and the car delivers you not to a lobby but to a pause. Low lighting, still air, and the quiet certainty that you are somewhere designed to be found.

02

The Welcome Pavilion

The morning starts at the pavilion — an open-sided space where coffee arrives without ceremony and the bay is already visible through the trees. The concierge knows your name and your preferred table. No queue, no key cards, no welcome speech. Just the beginning of whatever the day decides to be.

03

The Walk Home

Movement at SHER is on foot or by quiet electric cart — through paths lit at ground level, past ferns and stone walls and the shapes of buildings you will learn by memory. There is no car intrusion beyond the arrival hub. The walk back to your residence is part of the experience, not an inconvenience.

04

The Villa Morning

Wake to warm timber ceilings, open louvres and the sound of the bay below. The villa veranda is as much a room as any other — deep eaves, a reading chair, a view of treetops and water that changes with the light. Mornings at SHER are not rushed into. They are inhabited.

05

The Cottage Sanctuary

From the water, the cottage appears just as it was designed — set back into the hillside vegetation, present without imposing. Inside, the scale is deliberate: one sleeping suite, a private outdoor shower court, a veranda that faces the bay through a natural frame of trees. A place designed for those who measure quality not in volume, but in precision.

06

The Condo Owner’s View

The condominium owner begins the morning elevated — a private terrace open to the treeline and the shimmer of bay beyond. Timber ceiling, soft breeze through louvred panels, the smell of surrounding vegetation before the day fully opens. No neighbour visible. No noise except the birds. Easy access to the café, the trail, and the water below.

07

Nature Trail Ritual

The estate’s nature trail follows the mangrove edge — a conservation-managed path where birds stand undisturbed in the shallows and the ground smells of salt and earth. This is not a resort amenity. It is a privilege of being here. Owners walk it in the golden hour without encountering a crowd, because SHER was not built for crowds.

08

Bay Life

The bay is still in the mornings — calm enough to reflect the sky, warm enough to be in at any hour. The SHER canoe launches from a quiet edge of the estate, and the water asks for nothing back except your attention. An hour on Savannes Bay rewires something.

09

Evening Dining

Dinner at SHER is not a resort restaurant — it is an open pavilion above the treeline, with candles and a quiet service that knows when to arrive and when to disappear. Owners gather here without formality, drawn together by proximity and shared sensibility, as the bay holds the last light until well after the plates are cleared.

10

Night at SHER

At night, the estate settles into itself — low-glare lanterns along the stone paths, the shapes of rooflines against the dark canopy, and above it more stars than most owners have seen in years. The cottages and villas are swallowed by vegetation. SHER disappears into the landscape it was built to belong to. Nature whispers. Souls listen.

SHER may decline a prospective owner, subject to applicable law, where the proposed ownership relationship is not aligned with the sanctuary’s standards. Nothing binds either party until the executed agreements are in place.

    Ownership by International Buyers

    Saint Lucia regulates the acquisition of land by persons who are not nationals of Saint Lucia. In general terms, a non-national who wishes to acquire an interest in land is required to hold an Alien Landholding Licence before that interest can validly be taken. A company is assessed by reference to its shareholding and control, so a locally incorporated company with non-national ownership is ordinarily treated in the same way as an individual non-national.

    This is a licensing requirement, not an obstacle, and it is a routine part of Caribbean cross-border property transactions. It does, however, affect three things a buyer needs to plan for: the identity of the purchasing entity, the transaction timetable, and the total cost of acquisition.

    What to establish with your attorney:

    • Whether your intended purchasing entity — individual, company, or trust — requires a licence, and in whose name the licence must be issued.
    • The application requirements, supporting documents and character references you will need to assemble.
    • The realistic processing time, and how the purchase agreement handles the period before the licence is granted.
    • The licence fee, and how it interacts with stamp duty, property transfer charges and legal fees applicable to non-nationals.
    • What happens to your funds and to the agreement if a licence is refused or delayed beyond an agreed long-stop date.
    • Any conditions attached to the licence, including limits on the land area, permitted use, or subsequent transfer.
    • The position on inheritance, resale and assignment, and whether a further licence is required from a future buyer.

    SHER does not provide legal or tax advice and does not act for the buyer. The requirements, fees and processing times applicable to a licence are matters of Saint Lucian law and administrative practice and may change. Every non-national buyer must take independent advice from a Saint Lucia-qualified attorney before signing or transferring funds.

Payment Security

How Funds Are Controlled

No money is requested merely to register interest or enter the Register of Intent. Registration, the Ownership Review and entry to the Register are free.

Once an allocation is issued, Saint Lucia does not operate a statutory deposit-protection scheme for off-plan residential sales. Protection therefore comes from the structure of the transaction itself, and a buyer is entitled to see that structure in writing before any money moves.

The principles SHER applies

  • Reservation and deposit funds are received into a designated account identified in the executed legal documents, and nowhere else.
  • The receiving account, the signatories and the conditions for release are stated in writing before you are asked to transfer.
  • Funds are released against defined contractual events, not on request.
  • Refund rights, long-stop dates and the consequences of default are set out in the agreement, in both directions.
  • Every receipt is issued in writing and recorded against your cottage in the buyer portal.

What to require before transferring funds

  • Written confirmation of the receiving account, including the account holder's legal name.
  • Confirmation of whether funds are held as stakeholder or as agent, and by whom.
  • The precise circumstances in which the deposit becomes non-refundable.
  • Your attorney's independent confirmation that the arrangement is appropriate for your position.
Never

Do not transfer funds to an account communicated only by email, message or telephone, and do not transfer to any account that differs from the one named in your executed documents. Confirm account details independently, through a channel you have initiated, before every transfer.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I buy merely because I have the money?
No. SHER uses a curated Ownership Review. Financial readiness is one factor among several, and SHER may decline an application — subject to applicable law and non-discrimination requirements — where the proposed ownership relationship is not aligned with the sanctuary’s standards. Money alone does not create an entitlement to purchase.
What is the SHER Ownership Review?
A confidential, mutual review of readiness, intended use, values alignment and acceptance of the estate’s conservation controls. The criteria are issued on request. Not every application proceeds, and SHER is not obliged to give reasons.
Is this about Lot 890 or a wider collection?
Phase 1 is a demonstration-and-sales cottage on Lot 890, land already controlled by SHER. The wider collection of ten cottages is a phased future programme — the exact sites and release sequence are not yet fixed. The Lot 890 cottage is the current proof of product and the first available opportunity.
Is there a fee to register or join the Register of Intent?
No. Registration, the Ownership Review and entry to the Register of Intent are free and non-binding on both sides. No money is requested at any point before an allocation is issued and the reservation documentation is in front of you. A place in the Register cannot be purchased.
Can I choose my cottage, or its butterfly?
No. Cottages are released in phases and allocated to registrants in order. You may record a preference, but it cannot be guaranteed. Each butterfly identity is permanently attached to a specific cottage, so the identity comes with the allocation. Finishes, furnishings and artwork are then selected within that identity.
When do I see pricing?
Pricing is issued privately when an approved opportunity reaches release readiness and the applicant has progressed through the Ownership Review. It is not published, and it is not the basis on which applications are assessed.
I am not a Saint Lucian national. Can I buy?
Yes, subject to the licensing requirements described in the orientation package and in the buyer journey above. A non-national ordinarily requires an Alien Landholding Licence, and your attorney should confirm the position for your intended purchasing entity before you sign.
Are the images final?
No. All cottage images on this portal communicate owner-approved visual intent. They are not renderings of the final approved design and are subject to change. The signed legal documents, final drawings and specifications control what is delivered.
What protects my funds?
See “How Funds Are Controlled” above. No funds are requested before an allocation is issued. The legal documents must identify the receiving account, the basis on which funds are held, the release conditions and your refund rights. Obtain independent legal advice before transferring funds.
