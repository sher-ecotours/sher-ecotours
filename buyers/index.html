Closer Than You Think

Savannes Bay sits at the southern tip of Saint Lucia, 15 minutes by road from Hewanorra International Airport. For a guest arriving from Miami, London, Toronto, or New York, the journey from plane to bay is one of the shortest in the Caribbean — no two-hour transfer, no winding mountain road. Direct access to a protected wetland estate on arrival.

The Right Moment to Own Here

Savannes Bay is entering a period of considered, high-quality development. A major five-star resort is currently under construction on the shared oceanfront a short distance away — bringing international visitor traffic and hospitality infrastructure to a coastline that has historically been known only to those who looked for it.

SHER residences placed in the HOA rental pool are managed, maintained, and marketed to guests who have already chosen this part of Saint Lucia. Owners who prefer personal use retain priority access. The structure is designed to serve both.

This is not a speculative position. It is an early, well-located stake in an area that institutional hospitality investment has already validated.

The HOA Rental Pool

Owners who do not occupy their residence full-time may participate in the SHER HOA Rental Pool. SHER manages the property, the bookings, and the guest experience — to the same standard as the lodge. Owners receive income distributions and return to a fully maintained residence whenever they choose.

Full Property Management

SHER handles all guest bookings, housekeeping, maintenance, and arrival experience. There is nothing for the owner to coordinate. Your property is managed as part of the wider sanctuary offering.

Income Distribution

Rental income is distributed to participating owners on a pro-rata basis per the HOA pool agreement. Distribution terms, management fees, and owner priority periods are defined in the HOA documentation.

Owner Priority Access

Owners designate up to a defined number of months per year for personal use, during which the property is removed from the rental pool. Advance notice requirements and blackout periods apply per the HOA agreement.

Conservation Standards Maintained

The HOA maintains the estate’s conservation standards at all times. No modifications may be made to a property that compromise its materials, footprint, or covenant obligations without written agreement from SHER.
