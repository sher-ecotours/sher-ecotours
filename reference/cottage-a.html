Bold row is the approved standard build. Expanded options maintain the same concept, proportions and sanctuary experience while providing more generous interior space and veranda depth.

  • Spacious, airy and open — connected to nature from every room
  • Premium eco-luxury materials and craftsmanship throughout
  • Light, ventilation and views from multiple directions
  • Strong indoor–outdoor flow with full privacy from neighbouring units
  • Quiet service systems — no visible tanks, pumps or plumbing
  • Architecture that feels larger than its footprint
  • Natural daylight and soft warm evening light in all renders
  • No people in primary exterior images
  • Vegetation must be realistic and native to Saint Lucia
